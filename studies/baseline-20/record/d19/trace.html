Here is the way I think of what days have financial true ups:

Whatever final report date you have take the prior calc day --that is what is being trued up.
the only catch to this rule of thumb is weekends and holidays
in general mondays final follows the rule of thumb as it includes true ups for friday,

apply the rule of thumb to that date.

HOLIDAYS

if for example Tuesday was a holiday then Wednesdays final would have true ups for Monday
